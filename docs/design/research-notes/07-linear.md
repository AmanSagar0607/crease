# Linear — reference evidence

**Reference:** [Linear](https://linear.app/) (`linear.app`), a product development system for teams and agents. This is one visual/product reference, not an analytics competitor.

## Verifiable product facts

Linear’s homepage positions it for planning and building products and highlights intake/integrations, planning and monitoring, AI/automations, and build/review/ship workflows. It describes speed, reduced noise, and product-team workflows as benefits. [1]

Linear’s security page states SOC 2 Type II audits, GDPR commitment, ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification, workspace app approvals, audit logs covering important events over the last three months, identity controls, HTTPS/TLS 1.2 in transit, AES-256 at rest, and EU or US data-region selection. These are Linear claims, not independently validated here. [2]

Linear’s privacy policy describes categories of personal data it collects, including profile/contact, payment, device/IP, web analytics, and social-network data; it also describes service-provider sharing and cookies/analytics practices. Its current policy is dated November 5, 2026. [3]

The live pricing page lists Free ($0), Basic ($10/user/month billed yearly), Business ($16/user/month billed yearly), and Enterprise (custom, annual billing only). API and webhook access are included across all tiers; the page lists private teams and guests on Business and Enterprise. [4]

## Observed presentation and adaptable patterns

The homepage communicates product capability through product-like issue/activity and workflow examples, then organizes the story into concise capability sections. The fetched text representation does not establish exact colors, fonts, animations, or pixel-level layout; those should be treated as visual observations requiring direct visual inspection, not facts from the text extraction. [1]

For Zappa, safely adapt the principle of showing real, legible product evidence early: a compact dashboard preview, acquisition-source breakdown, and revenue attribution trail with explicit observed/unknown states. Borrow concise navigation and capability grouping, not Linear’s issue/cycle/team model, wording, logo, art, or distinctive page composition. Zappa’s trust content should accurately explain its own event collection, identity, retention, and access controls rather than imply Linear’s certifications or controls.
